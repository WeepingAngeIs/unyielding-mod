# Inkyeon motion integration

The approved v14 geometry, paired normal/shiny UV textures, shutter blink, playful idle, brisk battle idle, native cry and pose are included. The battle idle lasts 2.666666667 seconds versus the 8-second playful idle.

Walking and swimming add `walk_alignment` between the ground stance and the native quadruped gait. The original stance splays both rear hips by 2.5 degrees in yaw and roll, and rolls the rear feet by 3 degrees. Combining that splay with the gait's pitch rotation sends the feet through sideways arcs. The alignment cancels only those lateral angles while moving, so each rear leg swings in a forward/back plane. The native pitch, diagonal stepping phases, knee and ankle bends, stride speed and amplitude are retained. Idle and battle animations, model geometry and both textures are unchanged.

The review viewer also has browser JavaScript collision physics and falling ink/puddle effects. Those browser-only effects are not supported by this resource-pack export and are not claimed to run in Minecraft. They remain preserved in the local review checkpoint.
